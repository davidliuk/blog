# Public content boundary

This repository is the public portfolio and knowledge base.

Publish:

- Verified portfolio facts, published research, open-source projects, and contact details intended for public use.
- Original explanations, technical tutorials, and course-topic summaries that stand on their own.
- Research reading notes rewritten in the author's own words with sources linked.

Keep in the private `note` repository:

- Applications, interview records, recommendation letters, resume drafts, compensation, immigration, finance, health, relationships, and personal plans.
- Grades, student identifiers, named peer feedback, raw coursework submissions, answer keys, and unreleased research or collaboration details.
- Credentials, private infrastructure addresses, and unredacted logs or screenshots.

Before publishing a page, check the text, images, linked assets, generated site output, and Git diff. Moving or deleting a file here does not erase an earlier public Git commit or a deployed copy.
