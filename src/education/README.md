## How to Use This Archive

- Use each course overview as the map for its lectures, assignments, projects, and exam material.
- Treat grades, schedules, and administrative details as historical records rather than current course policy.
- Follow links into the curated knowledge base when you want a topic-centered explanation rather than semester notes.
- Course pages may mix English and Chinese because they preserve how the material was originally studied.
